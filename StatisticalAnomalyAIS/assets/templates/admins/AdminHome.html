AIS Dataset Overview

Maritime Cybersecurity | Anomaly Detection Research

📊 Research Summary

A global trend in the progressive digitalisation of the world is affecting different industries, including the maritime transport sector. Electronic navigation and autonomous sailing rely on advanced sensor systems such as GNSS, RADAR, and AIS.

Interference with these systems can compromise situational awareness and lead to incorrect navigation decisions. This project focuses on analyzing AIS data to identify anomalies based on:

  • Transmission timing behavior
  • Reported vs calculated speed
  • Speed variation patterns

A 24-hour AIS dataset recorded in Tallinn was statistically analyzed to understand real-world maritime behavior. The study revealed that relying on a single feature is insufficient for anomaly detection.

Combining multiple speed-based features significantly improves detection accuracy.

These findings contribute to improving the cybersecurity of maritime navigation systems by identifying vulnerabilities in AIS data.
